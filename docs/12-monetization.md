# Monetization

Charge users for installing your app. Set it up when creating the app (**Monetization** tab) or later: **Home → Created → ⋯ → Set up monetization**, then turn on **Enable monetization**.

## Pricing models

| Model | Access | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| One-time payment | Permanent after a single payment | Payment system, amount, optional % discount (users see old and new price) |
| Annual subscription | One year | Payment system, amount, optional % discount |

- Add up to **12** payment methods with **+ Add payment method**.
- Currency comes from the payment system settings.
- Payment systems are connected in **Account settings → Accept payments**: https://sendpulse.com/knowledge-base/account-settings/accept-payments

## CRM integration

Each purchase creates a deal in your SendPulse CRM. By default it lands in the first stage of your first pipeline; choose another pipeline and stage per payment status. The contact card gets a comment with the buyer's source, location, device and browser. Build Automation 360 flows on these deals for onboarding or renewal reminders.

## Payments

**Account settings → Accept Payments**: buyer name, app, price, last status update and payment status. Statuses come from the payment system.
