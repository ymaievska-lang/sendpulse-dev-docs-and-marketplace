# About SendPulse

## In one sentence

SendPulse is an **AI-driven email and chatbot marketing platform** with a built-in CRM: it helps businesses capture and nurture leads, convert them into customers, and drive repeat purchases from one workspace.

## Positioning

- **AI-first.** A built-in AI assistant works across every tool, and AI chatbots can run a conversation "from the first hello to checkout".
- **One platform instead of a tech stack.** Email, chatbots in every major messenger, CRM, automation, websites and courses share one contact base and one account. Tagline: "Run your business, not your tech stack."
- **Conversations as the core.** "Build customer loyalty, one conversation at a time": messenger chatbots and email are the main channels, and the CRM sits behind them.
- **Free to start.** A free plan with no time limit; no credit card required.

## The customer funnel SendPulse covers

| Stage | Promise | How |
|---|---|---|
| Capture | Never miss a lead | Leads and messages from every channel land in one place |
| Convert | Grow revenue from every lead | Automated outreach and chatbot flows guide contacts to purchase |
| Engage | Foster loyalty and drive repeat sales | Upsells, timely campaigns, feedback collection |

## Products

**Core:** Chatbots · Email campaigns · CRM · Automation 360

**Chatbot channels:** WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, TikTok, Viber, plus live chat on websites.

**More tools:** WhatsApp campaigns, transactional email (SMTP), SMS, Viber messages, web push, pop-ups, website and landing page builder, link-in-bio pages, online course builder (LMS), email verifier.

## AI

**AI assistant** inside SendPulse:

| Does | Examples |
|---|---|
| Creates | Campaign copy, chatbot flows, pop-ups, automations, website blocks |
| Interacts | Handles customer conversations 24/7, translates messages, understands voice messages |
| Assists | Creates CRM deals, logs call summaries, automates daily routines |
| Manages | Runs campaigns, chatbots, courses and CRM updates on the user's behalf |

**AI chatbots** answer beyond scripted flows using the business's prompts, reference content and web search, and can connect to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek.

**AI agents outside SendPulse** (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others) connect through the SendPulse MCP server at `https://mcp.sendpulse.com/mcp`. Details: https://sendpulse.com/features/mcp?utm_source=context7&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=dev-docs&utm_content=00-about-sendpulse

## Where the marketplace fits

The **SendPulse App Directory** is the app and template marketplace inside SendPulse. Users install apps that connect SendPulse to the tools they already use, such as eCommerce platforms, CRMs, payment systems, forms, CMSs and AI platforms. Developers list apps there to reach SendPulse's customer base, and SendPulse handles user acquisition. See [App Directory overview](01-overview.md).
